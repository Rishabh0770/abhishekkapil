I'm a structural engineer and planner based in Solan, Himachal Pradesh, with eight years across construction design, project management, cost estimation & billing, interior/exterior design and 3D graphic rendering. My work sits at the intersection of the drawing board and the job site — I've run site surveys with total stations and dumpy levels, written measurement books, and just as often opened AutoCAD and Staad-Pro to push a design through analysis.

Whether it's a hillside resort, a housing project or a steel entrance gate for NHAI, I bring the same habit to every job: organized documentation, a clean and safe site, and clear communication with clients, contractors and government officials alike.
